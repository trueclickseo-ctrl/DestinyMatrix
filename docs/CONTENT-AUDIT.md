# Souliography — Content Quality & RHLF Audit Report

## 1. RHLF Framework Applied
- **Research**: First-principles investigation into Tarot symbolism, Natalia Ladini's 2000s Destiny Matrix system, and classical Pythagorean numerology.
- **Human Interpretation**: Distinct editorial voices for each of the 22 Major Arcana, 365 birthday dates, and numerology numbers.
- **Layered Information**: Logical flow from Quick Answer -> Meaning -> Step-by-Step Calculation -> Real-World Vocation/Love -> Reflection Prompts.
- **Fresh Writing**: Banned generic AI buzzwords ("Whether you're...", "Unlock your potential...", "At its core...", "Ultimately...").

## 2. Rebuilt Content Datasets
- `src/data/arcana.ts`: 22 Major Arcana entries with historical context, constructive vs shadow expressions, vocation, money line, love line, and reflection prompts.
- `src/data/birthdays.ts`: 365 birthday profiles with date-specific seasonal context, day vibrations, primary Arcana links, and reflection prompts.
- `src/data/numerology-content.ts`: In-depth guides for numbers 1–9, 11, 22, 33 across all numerology calculators.
