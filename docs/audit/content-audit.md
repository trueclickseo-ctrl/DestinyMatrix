# Content Audit

## 1. Editorial Voice & Quality Assessment
- **Current Voice**: Contains AI template patterns, generic spiritual clichés ("Unlock your true potential", "Embrace your spiritual journey", "This powerful energy", "At its core", "Whether you're", "Ultimately").
- **Structural Uniformity**:
  - Arcana pages (22 routes): Identical section structure across all pages.
  - Birthday pages (365 routes): Programmatically generated by mapping `day % 22` to an Arcana, causing identical text for multiple dates (e.g. Feb 5, March 5, April 5).
  - Numerology number pages (288 routes): Driven by `calcContextMap` with generic 1-sentence templates.
- **Framing**: Presenting numerology as absolute factual statements in some places without clear non-dogmatic self-reflection disclaimers.

## 2. Mandatory Editorial Quality Gate Mandates
All content must be rebuilt using the **RHLF (Research, Human Interpretation, Layered Information, Fresh Writing)** method:
- **Research (R)**: Search intent, user questions, historical Tarot & Ladini 22 Arcana context.
- **Human Interpretation (H)**: Varied sentence rhythms, concrete real-life examples, non-dogmatic self-reflection framing, no generic AI buzzwords.
- **Layered Information (L)**: Layer 1 (Quick Answer) -> Layer 2 (Concept) -> Layer 3 (Math/Methodology) -> Layer 4 (Examples) -> Layer 5 (Nuances) -> Layer 6 (Internal Links).
- **Fresh Writing (F)**: Original editorial structures for every page family.
