Arcana 17: The Star

The Beacon of Hope & Inspired Talent

Chakra: Third Eye & Heart (Inspiration & Celestial Light)

Arcana 17 carries the vibration of divine inspiration, talent recognition, optimism, fame, and spiritual renewal.

Depicting the maiden pouring celestial waters under seven stars, Arcana 17 represents hope, inspiration, and cosmic guidance.
