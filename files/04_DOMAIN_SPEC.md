## 12. Content Cluster Pattern

The platform must support thousands of pages built from repeatable cluster patterns. Example — a single hub (e.g. "Life Path") naturally generates dozens of related guides:

Life Path → Meaning → Compatibility → Career → Love → Strengths → Weaknesses → Famous People → Best Careers → Life Purpose

This pattern applies across all 12 pillars, not just numerology — the Knowledge Graph and Taxonomy Engine (01_ARCHITECTURE.md) are what make each cluster's internal linking automatic rather than hand-built per hub.
